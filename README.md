## About

I build and validate software that businesses can rely on. My work sits at the intersection of **quality engineering**, **software development**, and **process design** — making sure what gets delivered matches the business rules, the data stays consistent, and releases don't break what already works.

I founded **[Skytalos](https://skytalos.com.br)**, a technology consultancy where I lead projects in **software development**, **QA automation**, and **process management** for companies that need dependable systems without the overhead of a large team.

My background in **Business Administration** (UniFAJ) and nearly a decade running data and process quality inside a construction group shape how I approach every project: understand the operation first, translate it into clear scope and acceptance criteria, then build and test against it. Currently pursuing a **B.Sc. in Software Engineering** (Anhanguera) and preparing for **ISTQB CTFL**.
